Aural

Hold Ctrl + Win, speak, release. Your words appear in whatever app has focus — transcribed on this PC, never uploaded.

Brand idea: speed, clarity and flowing water. Black, white and neutral grey only.

The listening pill

A 176 × 36 px capsule that floats above the taskbar while you dictate. Logo on the left, twelve level bars that move with your real voice.

  1. Listening — bars rise and fall with the microphone, from 90 Hz to 4 kHz.
  2. Processing — bars settle to dots; a soft highlight sweeps across them.
  3. Success — the dots join into one line, then the pill fades away.
  4. Error — a short grey label: “Mic blocked”, “No model”, “Copied — Ctrl+V”.
  5. Hidden — nothing on screen when you are not dictating.
