# Spike: Qwen3-ASR 0.6B

**Verdict: no-go for v0.2** (final for this version, per the plan). Runtime and license
hold; the benchmark shows no win: same accuracy as models Aural ships, much slower on the
processor, and only marginally faster on the graphics card where everything already fits
the dictation budget.

## Model

- `Qwen/Qwen3-ASR-0.6B`: an audio encoder feeding a Qwen3 language model that writes the
  transcript; 30 languages with auto-detect.
- Export tried: `handy-computer/Qwen3-ASR-0.6B-gguf` @
  `28c66fba077f1359a42b52c55b618a24cdb6e0e7`, file `Qwen3-ASR-0.6B-Q8_0.gguf` (850 MB,
  SHA-256 `f081b2d5…c319b5acd8`), verified after download. (`ggml-org/Qwen3-ASR-0.6B-GGUF`
  also exists, for llama.cpp's multimodal path.)

## Runtime

transcribe.cpp v0.2.4 (MIT; commit `4807edaf210d0d7e8a6f7fb2a44b65966a2797f0`), built from
source on this PC with Vulkan, as in the Nemotron spike. Integrating it would mean a third
worker family.

## Benchmark (this PC: Core Ultra 9 185H, RTX 4070 Laptop, driver 617.14)

Same method and 100 LibriSpeech clips as the Nemotron spike, English hint (`-l en`).

| runs on | WER | p50 ms | p95 ms | load ms | peak RAM MB |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| processor | 2.65% | 1,103 | 3,366 | 1,188 | 1,810 |
| graphics card (Vulkan) | 2.65% | 205 | 529 | 1,413 | 946 |

Catalog comparison on the same clips: Parakeet TDT v2 (processor) 2.51% / 279 ms; Whisper
large-v3-turbo (Vulkan) 2.56% / 295 ms; Distil-Whisper large-v3.5 (Vulkan) 3.17% / 283 ms.

- **Accuracy:** within the ±0.7-point margin of Parakeet and turbo; not better.
- **Speed:** about four times slower than Parakeet on the processor (the language model
  decodes token by token); on the graphics card about 90 ms faster than turbo at p50 but
  with a longer tail (p95 529 vs 390 ms).
- Its multilingual reach overlaps with Parakeet v3, already in the catalog, and Aural is
  English-first.
