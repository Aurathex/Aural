"Real audio" = `app/src-tauri/tests/live_audio.rs`: a LibriSpeech clip played into the
Steam Streaming Microphone loopback pair and recorded by Aural's own capture, streamed by
the app's live feeder to the release worker. Every run's final text held the whole
reference sentence once.

What this shows: phrase mode on a fast model gives the lowest wait after release (the
last phrase is the only thing left to read). Native streaming shows words sooner and more
steadily, but Moonshine's decoder re-reads everything encoded so far, so its final text
takes longer on long dictations (small: 2.6 s for 11 s of speech on this processor).

### Model comparison (2026-09-28)

`ai_cleanup_model_comparison` in `workers/stt-onnx/tests/ai_cleanup.rs`: 20 harder
dictations (long run-ons, several sentences, questions, lists, negation, names, numbers,
an email, code, a command, an instruction-like sentence, text already punctuated by the
speech model), each first tidied by Light, then by the model, then put through Aural's
unchanged check. Same PC, processor, 4 threads, 4-bit ONNX exports, same prompt.

| model (4-bit ONNX) | size | accepted | better than Light | mean | p95 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct** (shipped), old prompt | 786 MB | 17/20 | 9/20 | 1.2 s | 3.1 s |
| **Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct**, new prompt | 786 MB | **18/20** | 8/20 | 1.4 s | 3.5 s |
| Qwen3-0.6B | 919 MB | 16/20 | 8/20 | 1.7 s | 4.0 s |
| Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct | 1.79 GB | 14/20 | 9/20 | 2.6 s | 6.7 s |
| SmolLM2-1.7B-Instruct | 1.41 GB | 15/20 | 8/20 | 3.1 s | 7.4 s |

Every refusal was correct: a lowercase code capitalised ("gate b12" → "B12", "api" →
"API"), "180 dollars" turned into "$180", words dropped or changed ("and third" → "Third",
"check whether" → "check if", "I rotate" → "you rotate"). The bigger models reword more
and are refused more, and are 2–3 times slower, so Qwen2.5-0.5B stays. The new prompt
asks the model to keep capitals and never turn words into symbols, with one more worked
example; the original 12-case battery is still 12/12 (mean 1.3 s).

What the AI step adds over Light (which now ends sentences itself): commas, sentence
breaks inside run-ons, lists ("three things: milk, eggs, and bread") and capitals on
names and days. What it cannot do: change any word, so it never fixes a misheard word or
capitalises a name inside a refused answer.
