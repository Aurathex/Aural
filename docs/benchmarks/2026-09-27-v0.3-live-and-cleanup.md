# v0.3: live transcription and AI tidy-up on the reference PC

PC: Intel Core Ultra 9 185H, RTX 4070 Laptop 8 GB + Intel Arc, 15.4 GB RAM, Windows 11,
on AC power. All runs on the processor unless stated. Release builds.

| moonshine-streaming-small@cpu | 3.22% | 307 | 2,420 | 0.069 | 777 | 537 | 1.26% |

## Live text

| engine, mode | how measured | update interval | per update | final text after stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Moonshine Streaming tiny, native | engine test, 10.4 s clip fed in 320 ms pieces | 320 ms | 4–206 ms | 220 ms |
| Moonshine Streaming small, native | real audio through a virtual microphone, 11.4 s | 0.6–1 s | — | 2.6 s |
| Parakeet TDT v2 int8, phrases | worker test, two sentences with a 1 s pause | 320 ms | 62–668 ms | 122 ms |
| Parakeet TDT v2 int8, phrases | real audio through a virtual microphone, 11.4 s | ~0.4 s | — | 55 ms |
| Whisper tiny.en q8, phrases | worker test, two sentences with a 1 s pause | 320 ms | ≤ 445 ms | 378 ms |
| Moonshine Streaming small, native, long | engine test, 91 s of LibriSpeech back to back | 320 ms | ≤ 2.3 s (at phrase ends) | 1.3 s; last sentence intact (the previous one-stream version ran 30 min and lost it) |

"Real audio" = `app/src-tauri/tests/live_audio.rs`: a LibriSpeech clip played into the
Steam Streaming Microphone loopback pair and recorded by Aural's own capture, streamed by
the app's live feeder to the release worker. Every run's final text held the whole
reference sentence once.

What this shows: phrase mode on a fast model gives the lowest wait after release (the
last phrase is the only thing left to read). Native streaming shows words sooner and more
steadily, but Moonshine's decoder re-reads everything encoded so far, so its final text
takes longer on long dictations (small: 2.6 s for 11 s of speech on this processor).

## AI tidy-up

Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, 4-bit ONNX (793 MB), through the real ONNX worker
(`workers/stt-onnx/tests/ai_cleanup.rs`), 12 everyday dictations including a price, a date,
an email address, code identifiers, an IP address and port, a question and an
instruction-like sentence:

| | result |
|---|---|
| load | 3.5 s |
| accepted by Aural's check | 12 / 12 (also under the final, stricter same-words check) |
| mean time per dictation | 1.07 s (after caching the shared instructions; 1.96 s without) |
| numbers, email, code, IP kept exactly | yes (the check guarantees it; any change falls back to the light tidy-up) |

The int8 exports of Qwen2.5 0.5B ("What time is it at room 204?") and 1.5B ("I'm sorry,
but I can't assist with that.") answered instead of tidying and are not offered.
